# Commercial Real Estate Website Guide

Primary: #0F172A

Background: #FFFFFF
Surface: #F8FAFC
Text: #111827

## Typography

## Required Pages
- Home
- Property Listings

- Market Insights
- About

- Advanced Search
- Filters
- Property Comparison

- Reviews

Make the website look comparable to PropertyGuru, Savills, Knight Frank, or Sotheby's Realty.
